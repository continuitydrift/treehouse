    Treehouse Project

    A year ago I became the proud but somewhat terrified custodian of the Treehouse at Dancing Rabbit: A one-bedroom off-grid cabin held above the Missouri prairie like a Baba Yaga hut by twelve Osage posts.

    It is a beautiful--some would say ridiculous--structure, that the previous proprietor had been renting out as a three-season Bed and Breakfast, despite lacking siding, heating, insulation, or electricity.

    My goal is to make it livable, with functional systems, by this winter. Currently, I'm looking for ways to mitigate the summer heat (an 'aluminet' 20x16 heat-shielding shade cloth should be arriving tomorrow to soften the greenhouse effect of the west-facing wall of windows). The other immediate project is ordering and installing a solar kit.

    This website, for now, is to solicit feedback on the components I'm about to order. You are the crowds, and I want your wisdom. Or, to put it differently: one of the advantages of living in a cooperative ecovillage, and having smart and ecologically minded friends, is the availability of good advice, and this is my way of asking for it.

    I'm hoping to order everything in the next few days, so if you do want to email me or talk in person, please do so soon.

    Solar System — Asking for Feedback

    The problem of choosing an off-grid solar setup for a small, stilt-supported cabin has a particular combination of my own lack of expertise, and significant expense, that makes me nervous enough to sincerely request any input you might have.

    Note: the selection of components, as well as the reasoning, is the result of my using Artificial Intelligence (mostly Claude), and not my own understanding. This is one of the reasons I would like humans, whose opinions I value, in the loop.

    The Gear

    Note: Component name links to site where I plan to order from.

    Component Spec Why
    Solar panels 2× Renogy 200W I plan to use solar to run some LED lights, charge a phone and laptop, and run some tools with minor draw like a small fan. 400W should be enough for this, but not anything like a fridge or mini-split. Two panels rather than one gives me more options for placement and replacement. Should pair well with the 100Ah/12V battery.
    Charge controller 40A MPPT Right size for the panel array, MPPT should get more power than cheaper PWM controllers. The 400W array should only draw roughly 33A max at 12V
    Battery 100Ah LiFePO4 LiFePO4 will last longer than a lead-acid battery. Also lighter and smaller.
    Inverter 2000W pure sine Apparently more efficient and safer than modified sine.
    Battery monitor Victron SmartShunt This should let me monitor the battery's state of charge (the current going in and out). Hooks up with Bluetooth app to analyze data and trends.

    What I'd like feedback on

    Mostly I just don't want to do anything stupid, or waste money. Once I have the system, my father has offered to help install it, but the more eyes and hands on this project the better I will feel.

    How to weigh in

    Email, call, or talk to me in person! If you don't have time to reach out in the relevant window, or don't feel like you have anything to add on the solar question, don't worry: there will be plenty of other opportunities to offer advice or criticism in the future. Future challenges include:

    • Finishing felt paper and lath strips (70% complete)
    • Putting up White Oak siding (on order from Burr's sawmill). For safety, I'll probably need to set up scaffolding rather than working from community ladders. By the way, I have two community ladders at Treehouse--let me know if you need them.
    • Insulation. I'm thinking blown cellulose. Needs to go in three walls and the ceiling.
    • Heating. A large wood stove with a box around it was generously gifted, but I'm not sure if it will work, where to locate it, and how to install the flue, etc.

    I plan to use this webpage to document progress, and to ask the community for input on other aspects of the project. Let me know if you want to be kept up to date, even if you don't currently have any feedback. Thank you in advance for any and all assistance!
