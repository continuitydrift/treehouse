# 2026-11-23

It's writing group night, and I'm in the mercantile, wondering if I remember how to upload changes to a webpage. 

I almost didn't have lights tonight in the treehouse. The last bits of insulation are in the corner by the door, where the electrical is fixed to plywood boards, the AC already on the wall but with the inverter not working, the DC getting wired up this evening, which is why I almost had to rely on candles. It was getting dark, and I was trying to hook the cable from the battery, now running through holes in the 2x4s of the wall, up through a shelf, and out through the plywood, into a main 80 Amp fuse with a disconnect switch. The little "o" connector barely didn't fit on the bolt of the fuse. What I needed were those "u" shaped ones. There were some in the machine shed, but I didn't have time. They weren't in the cloth bag with all the fiddly electrical bits, or the Star Wars C3PO toy box I use for tools. I'd seen some on the floor, somewhere, but it was getting dark.

I like working with my hands. I'm surprised by the dexterity needed for things like wiring, or installing batts of insulation. There were moments of the paper fronting of the fiberglass tearing free from staples while I was trying to take the weight off with my head, on the top rung of a ladder, where difficulty had me shouting curses as loud as I could into the respirator. Similarly, trying to get a wire to tighten into tiny washers and nuts after dozens of failed attempts had me questioning my life decisions. I decided to skip the disconnect switch and bus bars, splicing the main cable into what was probably too small AWG wire, so it could directly reach the little fuse box I was using before, where I just pull the 20 amp fuses out in lieu of switches. 

Beans came by, but when I was too busy to give her food, left again.

Sparks. The lights came on, red and orange. I make bean and cheese quesadillas, using the pot for a plate. Watch an old Popeye Cartoon I downloaded from archive.org.

In the continuity drift novel set farthest in the "past", telepaths take up residences in the boles of trees, which act like psychic stages or amplifiers, broadcasting their thoughts like low-range radios. You can't cut any of the branches, but may use those that fall to make, among other things, large drums. The larger the tree, the deeper the sound.

This seems too portentous, or pretentious, not having written for a while. There is more to explain, threads leading out like strips of glitchy LEDs, context and qualifications.
